## The problem

Coastal Construction Services had no website and no Google Business Profile. Someone searching for dirt work around New Iberia had no way to find the company, and no easy way to reach it.

## What we built

We started from nothing and built the whole presence:

- Branding and a full website with custom animation
- Service pages for the work Coastal does
- Guide pages that answer what people search for before hiring
- A flood zone lookup and a checklist for new land buyers
- A quote form that goes straight to email, plus online booking through Cal.com
- A Google Business Profile, set up and managed

## How it runs now

The site launched in early 2026 and has been upgraded steadily since. We handle local SEO, hosting, maintenance and the Google Business Profile, so the owner can stay on the job and doesn't have to think about any of it.
