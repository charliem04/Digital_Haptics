## The problem

CTL's website dates from when the business first went online. The company has grown past what that site can show or do, and a roofer's site has a lot of work to do: taking leads, explaining financing, recruiting crews and staying useful during storm season.

## What we built

This is a working demo of a complete rebuild. It isn't CTL's official website. It includes:

- More than 20 pages covering services, work, financing and the company
- A form that goes to email, plus Calendly booking
- A hiring portal
- A financing portal with APR estimators
- A weather radar covering the local area
- A project gallery with a CMS, so new work is easy to add

## Where it stands

We're in talks with CTL about taking this live. If it goes ahead, we'd handle local SEO, hosting and maintenance from there. Until it launches there are no results to report, so this page shows the scope of the build.
