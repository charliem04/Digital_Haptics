## The problem

Darren Aucoin's Plumbing had a Google Business Profile, but no website. Its online home was a Facebook page that had gone quiet, so people who found the business had nowhere current to learn about it or ask for service.

## What we built

The job called for something simple that works, so that's what we built:

- A clear one-page site covering what the company does
- A lead form that goes straight to email
- Call and request-service buttons within easy reach on a phone

## How it runs now

The site launched in May 2026. We handle local SEO, hosting and maintenance, and it has brought in more than 30 new leads since launch.
