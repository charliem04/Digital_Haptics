## The job

Matt Guidry is a video producer and animator. He writes, shoots, edits and animates, mostly for brands and healthcare teams: logo animations, documentary stories about real people, and motion graphics.

A video producer's site has one job before anything else: show the work, fast. Then it has to turn a viewer into a project, with enough detail that Matt knows what he's quoting.

## What we built

A single page that reads like a reel, from first frame to contact:

- The showreel up front, framed like a live camera feed
- Twelve pieces of work in four groups: logo animation, documentary, motion graphics and comedy skits, each tagged with its resolution and runtime
- A short "behind the edit" section in Matt's own words
- A project-brief form that asks for budget, type of work, deadline and details, with spam protection built in
- Call and email buttons that copy Matt's details in one tap
- Terms and privacy pages
