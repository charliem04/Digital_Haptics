Most trades businesses don't need a fancy website. They need one that does a few jobs well, for someone standing in their yard with a phone in one hand. Here's what matters, in the order it matters.

## A phone number you can tap

If someone has to hunt for your number, you've already started losing them. It should be at the top of every page, and on a phone it should call you with one tap. If you can, keep a call button on screen as they scroll.

## A short way to reach you when you're busy

You can't answer every call from a roof or under a house. A short form catches the customer who would have hung up and tried the next company. Ask only for what you need: name, phone, and what's going on. Have it go straight to an email you actually check.

## A clear list of what you do

A customer should know within seconds whether you handle their problem. Give each main service its own section or page, in the words a customer would use. People search for "water heater repair," not "residential plumbing solutions."

## Where you work

Name the towns you serve. It tells a customer you'll come to them, and it helps search engines connect your business to local searches. Only list places you really work.

## Photos of real jobs

Stock photos of smiling models in clean hard hats fool nobody. Your truck, your crew and your finished work build more trust than anything you could write. A phone photo of a real job beats a polished fake one.

## Proof you're legitimate

Include whatever is true for your business: years in the trade, licenses, insurance, certifications and real customer reviews. Never exaggerate these. In a small community, people will find out.

## Speed on a phone

Most of your visitors are on a phone, and many are on a weak signal. A slow site loses them before they ever see your number. Big images and piled-on plugins are the usual culprits.

## What you can skip

You probably don't need a long company history on the home page, a sliding banner, a chat bot or a blog you'll never update. Every extra thing is one more distraction between the customer and your phone number.
